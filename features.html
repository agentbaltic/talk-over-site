Features

TalkOver. The teleprompter you've always wanted.

TalkOver is a teleprompter tool that follows along as you speak. And it's full of awesome features. No subscription, no data in the cloud.

Everything TalkOver does.

The script follows your voice.

No timer to guess at. No speed dial to keep nudging. Start speaking and the words come to you. Pause, and it waits. Wander off-script and it picks you back up when you come back to the page. Speech recognition runs entirely on your Mac — it works with the Wi-Fi off, and no account is involved.

Read it your way.

Three ways to move through a script, switchable mid-take: let your voice drive it, run a steady timed crawl, or work it by hand with the arrow keys. Text size, column width, line height, paragraph spacing and read-line height are all yours to set.

Float Over.

A transparent, always-on-top window you drag anywhere and size to fit — park it right under your webcam so your eyes stay where they belong, and leave Zoom, Meet, Teams, Keynote or your browser fully usable underneath. Screen Opacity sets how see-through it is, from a barely-there tint to solid, and a 100% button puts it back in one press. While it is on, your clicks pass through to whatever is behind, so you can keep working with the script still in view — the bar, the settings and the window's own title bar stay clickable throughout. The script is kept off a shared screen by default, so the people on the call see your slides and not your words.

Move the script without touching anything.

Voice-following keeps pace with you, but sometimes you need to jump. Say TalkOver page up or TalkOver paragraph down, TalkOver back or TalkOver next, TalkOver restart or TalkOver end — and TalkOver pause and TalkOver resume to stop and start the following itself.

The name has to come first, which is what keeps an ordinary sentence from moving the script mid-take. It all runs on your Mac, like the rest of the listening.

Record yourself, while you read.

Recording turns TalkOver into a self-recording setup: your camera and microphone capture you while you read, so you are looking into the lens rather than down at your notes. Made for tutorials, screencasts, product demos and training materials. Choose your camera, microphone and resolution, and a three-second countdown gets you ready. Takes save straight to your Downloads folder as you record — the script, the controls and the settings are never burned into the video, so the file is simply you on camera. While it runs, a small red indicator shows the elapsed time; drag it anywhere, or shrink it to a dot in the corner.

Bring your script in, keep it exactly as written.

Paste your text or drop in a .txt, .md, .markdown or .rtf file, up to 2 MB. TalkOver keeps what you gave it word for word, and prepares a separate reading copy that quietly drops the parts you were never meant to say out loud — notes to self, headings, bracketed stage directions. You read clean prose; your original stays untouched underneath.

Every save keeps the last one.

Every save is a new numbered version, and none is ever removed. Earlier drafts stay listed beside the script: open one to read it without disturbing anything, or bring it back — which adds it as the newest version rather than deleting the work that came after it.

It remembers where you were.

Leave the presenter to fix a typo, come back, and TalkOver puts you on the same line. It holds your place per script, using the words either side of that line too — so even an edit that shifts the text around still finds the spot you were reading.

Yours, on your own Mac.

TalkOver is a single Mac app with a small menu-bar icon and no cloud behind it. Your scripts, your voice and your recordings stay in your own folder on your own machine, and everything works with no connection at all. Replace the app and your library is exactly where you left it.
