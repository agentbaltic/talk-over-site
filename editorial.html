About Us

Why Talk Over?

I've always had a love-hate relationship with teleprompters. Having used them mostly for YouTube videos, I've never had someone scroll them for me while I talk.

Most teleprompter software scrolls by itself, and is somehow always too fast or too slow, or needs to be scrolled manually.

More recently, there have been some online tools that listen to your voice and scroll through the text for you. But those have monthly subscriptions, and they send your data to the cloud.

I built Talk Over to get rid of the hassle of a subscription and the data concerns about having a cloud-based tool listening to you talk.

Talk Over Version 1 has a one-time cost and is good for a lifetime. When Version 2 comes out, all Version 1 users will get an upgrade discount.

No subscriptions, no data going to the cloud. Anything you do with Talk Over stays on your Mac. And it's yours forever.
