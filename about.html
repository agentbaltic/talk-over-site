Why TalkOver?

We've always had a love-hate relationship with teleprompters. Having used them mostly for YouTube videos, we never had someone to scroll one for us while we talked.

Most teleprompter software scrolls by itself, and it's somehow always too fast or too slow, or it needs to be scrolled manually.

More recently, some online tools started listening to your voice and scrolling the text for you. But those charge a monthly subscription, and they send your voice to the cloud to do it.

We built TalkOver to get rid of both problems — the subscription, and the discomfort of a cloud tool listening to us talk. It runs on your Mac. It costs once. That's it.

TalkOver Version 1 is a one-time purchase, good for as long as you own the Mac it's on. When Version 2 arrives, everyone who bought Version 1 gets a discount on it.

No subscriptions. No data leaving your Mac. It's yours, for good.
